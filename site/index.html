Saving is easy. Doing is the hard part.

Most save-it-later apps are very good at the first half. Silo is built for the second: the things you saved should turn into things you did.

  1. Save

    Share any YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram or Reddit link to Silo. Drop in screenshots and notes. There's nothing to file by hand.

  2. Sort

    Every save is titled, tagged, thumbnailed and filed into a stack, so you find it by what it is, not by where you left it.

  3. Do

    Silo notices when you're free and what's nearby, then offers the right thing at the right time. One tap puts it on your calendar.

Built to be used, not hoarded.

Your library stays on your phone.

What you saved, where you go and what your week looks like is the most personal data there is. Silo is built so it doesn't have to live on someone else's server.

A few things you choose to run, such as saving a link, analysing an image or asking the assistant, pass through Silo's backend and are discarded. The privacy policy lists every one.

  • Saves, notes and screenshots are stored on your device.
  • No ad SDKs, no analytics product and no data brokers.
  • Your location never leaves your phone.
  • Sync, accounts and subscriptions are optional, and off until you turn them on.
